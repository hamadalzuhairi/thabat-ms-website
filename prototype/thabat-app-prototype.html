Design notes

  • Tremor-friendly: every target is 48dp or larger with 8dp gaps. No swipe-only, slider or timed actions.
  • Meaning is never colour alone: each status has words, an icon and a shape (circle, diamond, cross, triangle).
  • "No data" is always shown as missing, never as normal.
  • Arabic-first right-to-left layout. Charts stay left-to-right.
  • Calm, plain words. The app never diagnoses; it says "Please contact your MS team".
  • Safety guidance stays visible on Today, whatever the data shows.
